MORE THAN JUST A COURT

The basketball court is a place to practise your skills, challenge your friends and enjoy an active game outdoors. Players can work on shooting, dribbling, passing and footwork while making the most of the open space.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE

Whether you are practising alone or playing with a group, the court gives everyone the opportunity to get involved. Bring a basketball, choose your style of play and enjoy an energetic outdoor activity.

Before you visit

  1. Check the location and opening information.
  2. Bring a basketball and share the court with other visitors.
  3. Respect other visitors and keep the area clean.
