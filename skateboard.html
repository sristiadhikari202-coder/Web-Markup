Skateboard Park

Location: Leeghwaterpark, 1445 AR Purmerend

An outdoor skatepark with ramps, rails and obstacles for skateboarders to practise their skills. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced skater, it is a great place to learn new tricks and meet other skaters.

A SPACE TO RIDE

The skateboard park gives riders a dedicated outdoor space to practise, experiment and develop their skating skills. Ramps, rails and other features create opportunities to work on balance, turns, jumps and tricks.

LEARN, PRACTISE, PROGRESS

Every skater starts somewhere. Beginners can build confidence with basic movements, while more experienced riders can challenge themselves with new tricks and combinations. The park is a place to keep practising and enjoy the freedom of skating.

Before you visit

  1. Check the location and opening information.
  2. Wear a helmet and check ramps for hazards before skating.
  3. Respect other visitors and keep the area clean.
