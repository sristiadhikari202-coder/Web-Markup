A PLACE TO PLAY

The football field offers an open space where players can practise, play matches, and enjoy the sport with friends. Whether you are working on your passing, shooting, or teamwork, the field provides room to stay active and improve your game.

SPACE FOR EVERY PLAYER

From casual kickabouts to organised training, the field can be enjoyed by players of different ages and experience levels. The surrounding open area also makes it a welcoming place to spend time outdoors and stay active.

Before you visit

  1. Check the location and opening information.
  2. Bring your ball, comfortable sports shoes and enough water.
  3. Respect other visitors and keep the area clean.
