TRAIN IN THE OPEN AIR

The outdoor fitness area provides a simple and accessible way to exercise outside. Use the equipment for strength, balance, flexibility and bodyweight exercises while creating a workout that suits your own level.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE

You do not need a gym membership to stay active. A short workout can be added to your daily routine, whether you are warming up, doing a full-body session or simply taking a break to move.

Before you visit

  1. Check the location and opening information.
  2. Bring water and start with a gentle warm-up.
  3. Respect other visitors and keep the area clean.
